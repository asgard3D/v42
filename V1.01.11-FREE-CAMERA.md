# V1.01.11 — Free Camera / Sky View

Based on V1.01.10 Attack Timeline.

## Changes
- Horizontal camera yaw remains unrestricted for full 360-degree rotation.
- Desktop right-mouse camera pitch changed from downward-only to free vertical range `-1.25 .. +1.25` radians.
- Mobile two-finger camera pitch uses the same free vertical range.
- `updateCamera()` accepts negative pitch so the player can tilt the camera upward and see the sky.
- No changes to Character Model V2, movement, combat, attack timeline, weapons, maps, or skill-book rules.

## Safety
Three.js remains authoritative for the visible character/camera; PlayCanvas bridge remains non-authoritative.
