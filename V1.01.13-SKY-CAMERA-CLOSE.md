# V1.01.13 — Sky Camera Close

Based on V1.01.12.

- Looking upward smoothly pulls the third-person camera closer, MMORPG-style.
- Horizontal 360-degree camera remains unchanged.
- Upward look uses a smooth distance curve instead of an abrupt zoom.
- Camera remains above the ground and does not penetrate the floor.
- At extreme upward pitch, the look target is raised so the player can see the sky without forcing the camera through the floor.
- Character Model V2, movement, combat, weapons, PlayCanvas bridge, and class skill-book drop rules are unchanged.
