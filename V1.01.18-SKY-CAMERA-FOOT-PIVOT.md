# V1.01.18 — RPG Sky Camera Foot Pivot Fix

- Camera vertical pivot is the character shoes/feet, not the head.
- Negative pitch (looking upward) moves the camera toward the low foot/ankle area.
- Looking higher progressively shortens camera distance.
- Highest sky angle gives a low, near-first-person upward view.
- Camera remains above the ground plane; it can be below the character's feet.
- Existing Character Model V2 / equipment / PlayCanvas bridge / Three.js fallback preserved.
