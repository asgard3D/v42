# V1.01.24 — First-Person Hat-Top Penetration

- First-person camera endpoint raised from 1.78 to 2.02 world units above player base.
- First-person look target raised to preserve clear sky visibility.
- At first-person state, the complete Character Model V2 visual stack is hidden in both Three.js fallback and PlayCanvas active renderer.
- This prevents hair, hat/helmet, body, clothes, cloak, weapon and offhand from occluding the camera.
- Third-person camera and all gameplay/equipment state remain unchanged.
